ID: FR-004
Type: Functional
Description: The system shall allow an Account Holder to redeem available universal exchange credits for an eligible digital gift card.
Priority: High
Acceptance Criteria: Pass: A valid redemption request with sufficient credits creates a gift-card voucher. Fail: Redemption is rejected when the Account Holder has insufficient credits or the requested gift card is unavailable.
Rationale: Gift-card redemption is a core purpose of the digital wallet platform.
