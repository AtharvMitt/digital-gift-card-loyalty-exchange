ID: NFR-001
Type: Performance & Security
Description: Point conversion and gift card redemption operations shall complete within 250 ms under the defined normal operating load while maintaining zero ledger balance drift.
Priority: High
Acceptance Criteria: Pass: Benchmarking tests demonstrate that the operations complete within 250 ms and wallet balances remain consistent under simulated peak load. Fail: The latency target is exceeded or any ledger balance inconsistency occurs.
Rationale: Fast processing and accurate financial records are essential for a trustworthy exchange platform.
