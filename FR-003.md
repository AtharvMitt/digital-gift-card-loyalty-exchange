ID: FR-003
Type: Functional
Description: The system shall display the applicable point-to-credit conversion rate and the Account Holder's available universal exchange credit balance before redemption.
Priority: Medium
Acceptance Criteria: Pass: The current conversion rate and available balance are displayed correctly. Fail: Redemption information is not displayed when the required wallet data is unavailable.
Rationale: Account Holders need accurate conversion and balance information before making redemption decisions.
