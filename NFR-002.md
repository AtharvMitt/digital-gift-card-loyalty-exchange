ID: NFR-002
Type: Security
Description: The system shall protect digital voucher codes against unauthorized reuse, tampering, and fraudulent redemption.
Priority: High
Acceptance Criteria: Pass: Tampered, expired, or previously redeemed vouchers are rejected and valid vouchers can be redeemed only once. Fail: A modified or previously redeemed voucher is accepted.
Rationale: Strong voucher protection is necessary to prevent fraud and financial loss.
