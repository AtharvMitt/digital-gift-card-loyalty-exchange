ID: FR-005
Type: Functional
Description: The system shall generate a unique single-use digital voucher code and lock the voucher against duplicate redemption.
Priority: High
Acceptance Criteria: Pass: A unique voucher with a cryptographic checksum is generated and can be redeemed only once. Fail: An expired or previously redeemed voucher is rejected.
Rationale: Single-use voucher locking prevents duplicate redemption and reduces fraud.
